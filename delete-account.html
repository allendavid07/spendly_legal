Delete Your Spendly Account — Spendly Effective date: 25 July 2026 · Developer: Tidybitz This page explains how to delete your Spendly account and the data associated with it. 1. Delete from within the app Open Spendly, then go to: Settings → Account → Delete account. This recursively removes your Firestore data, Penny rate-limit records, your Firebase Authentication account, and your local Spendly financial data. This action cannot be undone. 2. Request deletion without the app If you no longer have the app installed or cannot sign in, email tidybitzminds@gmail.com with the subject "Delete Spendly Account" from the email address associated with your account. State that you are requesting deletion of your Spendly account and associated data. Tidybitz may ask you to verify account ownership before completing the request. 3. What gets deleted - Account profile (name, email address, optional profile photo) - Financial records (transactions, budgets, goals, debts, accounts, insurance policy records) stored in Firestore - Penny rate-limit and usage records - Locally stored app data on your device Anonymised community-learning vote totals cannot be linked back to an account and are not affected by account deletion. 4. Timing Requests submitted through the app take effect immediately. Requests submitted by email are processed after we verify account ownership. 5. More information For the full details on what data Spendly collects and how it is used, see the Privacy Policy: https://allendavid07.github.io/spendly_legal/ For questions about this process, contact tidybitzminds@gmail.com.
